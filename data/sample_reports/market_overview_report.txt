GLOBAL TECHNOLOGY MARKET OVERVIEW REPORT — Q4 2024
Prepared by: Market Research Division
Date: November 2024

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The global technology market continues to demonstrate robust growth in 2024, with total addressable market
estimated at $5.8 trillion, representing a 12.4% year-over-year expansion. Five key segments are driving
this growth: Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Infrastructure, Cybersecurity, E-commerce, and Financial Technology.
Enterprise digital transformation remains the primary demand driver across all segments, with AI adoption
reaching an inflection point that is reshaping competitive dynamics across every industry vertical.

MARKET SIZE AND GROWTH

The global AI software and services market reached $235 billion in 2024, up 41% from 2023. This remarkable
growth rate is driven by generative AI adoption, which has moved from experimental pilots to production
deployments at scale. Over 68% of Fortune 1000 companies now run at least one generative AI application in
production, compared to just 24% in 2023.

Cloud infrastructure spending totalled $720 billion globally in 2024, a 22% increase year-over-year.
Hyperscaler revenues — AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud — account for 67% of total cloud spending. The
remaining 33% is distributed among regional providers and private cloud deployments. Notably, AI workloads
now constitute 31% of all cloud compute consumption, up from 14% in 2023, fundamentally shifting workload
mix and infrastructure architecture priorities.

The global cybersecurity market reached $215 billion in 2024, growing at 13.8% annually. This growth is
being driven by the rising cost and frequency of cyberattacks, with the average data breach cost reaching
$4.88 million according to IBM Security's 2024 report. Regulatory requirements including NIS2 in Europe
and proposed SEC rules in the US are also compelling increased security spending across enterprises.

E-commerce global sales are tracking to $6.3 trillion in 2024, a 9% increase over 2023. Mobile commerce
leads with a 62% share of total transactions. The entry of TikTok Shop into the US market and the rapid
expansion of social commerce channels are creating new competitive pressures for established marketplaces.

COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

The enterprise technology market is characterised by a two-tier competitive structure. Established platform
providers — TechAlpha Corp, Apex Digital Systems — hold the largest market shares but face growing
competition from agile, cloud-native challengers such as NovaTech Solutions and CloudSpark Inc.

TechAlpha Corp leads the market with a 28.5% share, bolstered by its October 2024 acquisition of DataSense
AI for $1.2 billion. The company's strategy of building vertically-integrated AI solutions for healthcare,
finance, and manufacturing has delivered consistent enterprise wins. However, its premium pricing creates
an opening for challengers in the mid-market segment.

NovaTech Solutions has emerged as the fastest-growing established player, reaching 21.3% market share
through a developer-led growth strategy. Its release of NovaTech LLM 3.5 — which achieves top-5 scores
on industry benchmarks — and its $650 million Series E funding round at a $9.1 billion valuation signal
its intent to challenge TechAlpha directly.

EMERGING TRENDS

Three trends stand out as particularly significant for market participants heading into 2025:

First, the shift to agentic AI architectures. Enterprises are moving beyond single-model deployments
to multi-agent systems capable of autonomous task execution. This represents a fundamental architectural
shift that will require new tooling, monitoring, and governance frameworks.

Second, open-source AI model adoption is accelerating. Enterprise adoption of open-source models
has grown from 32% to 58% in the past 12 months, driven by data privacy concerns, cost control, and
the desire to avoid vendor lock-in. This trend benefits providers who offer hybrid deployment models.

Third, AI regulation is becoming a competitive differentiator. The EU AI Act, US executive orders on AI,
and sector-specific guidelines are creating compliance requirements that favour established vendors with
mature governance frameworks.

OUTLOOK

The technology market is projected to grow 14.2% in 2025, reaching approximately $6.6 trillion. AI will
remain the primary growth catalyst, with enterprise AI software spending forecast to grow 52% to $358 billion.
Cloud infrastructure growth will moderate to approximately 18% as the market matures, while cybersecurity
spending is expected to accelerate to 16% growth driven by increasing regulatory mandates and the
proliferation of AI-powered attack vectors.

Companies that successfully combine AI capabilities with robust data governance, multi-cloud flexibility,
and demonstrated ROI will be best positioned to capture premium enterprise contracts in this dynamic market.
